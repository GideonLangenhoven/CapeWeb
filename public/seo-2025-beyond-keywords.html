SEO in 2025: Beyond Keywords

How to Rank in the Era of AI Overviews and Semantic Search.

01

The Death of "10 Blue Links"

Users aren't typing keywords anymore; they're asking questions. Google's AI looks for comprehensive answers, not just keyword matches.

Information Gain: Google rewards content that adds something NEW to the conversation. Rehashed content will not rank.

The New Ranking Factors

  • E-E-A-T: Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust.
  • User Signals: High dwell time = High ranking.
  • Topic Clusters: deeply covering a subject area.
02

The Topic Cluster Model

Stop writing isolated blog posts. Build "Pillar Pages" that cover a broad topic comprehensively, then link out to specific "Cluster Content."

"SEO isn't dead, but gaming the system is. The future belongs to brands that publish expert-led content."
