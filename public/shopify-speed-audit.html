Shopify Speed Audit

Why Your Store is Leaking Money and How to Fix It.

01

The 3-Second Rule

53% of mobile users abandon a site if it takes longer than 3 seconds to load. Speed is currency in e-commerce.

Walmart Data: Every 1 second of improvement in load time resulted in a 2% increase in conversions.

Common Speed Killers

  • App Overload: Too many scripts running.
  • Huge Images: Uncompressed banners.
  • Bloated Themes: Poorly coded templates.
02

The Audit Checklist

1. Run a Lighthouse Score in Chrome DevTools.
2. Compress all images to WebP format.
3. Remove unused apps and their ghost code.
4. Minify CSS and JS files.

"Treat your site speed like your store's hygiene. Keep it clean, keep it fast, and watch your conversion rate climb."
