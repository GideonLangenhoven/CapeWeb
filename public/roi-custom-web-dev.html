The ROI of Custom Web Development

Why Templates Are Costing You Money and How Custom Code Drives Revenue.

01

Speed is a Feature

Templates are built to sell to everyone, which means they are packed with bloat. Custom sites are built for performance.

The Data: Amazon found that every 100ms of latency cost them 1% in sales. A custom site loads only what it needs, ensuring lightning-fast performance that Google loves.

Template vs. Custom

  • Bloat: Templates load unused scripts.
  • Security: WordPress themes are frequent hack targets.
  • Scalability: Custom code grows with your business without breaking.
02

Conversion-Centered Design

A template forces your content into a box. Custom development builds the box around your content.

"A template is an expense; it solves an immediate problem but depreciates. Custom development is an investment that appreciates over time."

We design strategic layouts where Call-to-Actions (CTAs) are placed exactly where user attention flows, drastically increasing lead generation.
