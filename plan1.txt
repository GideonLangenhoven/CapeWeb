# Website Content & Structure Plan
Based on the high-energy, automation-focused "CapeWeb" Home page.

## 1. Core Brand Theme
**"Websites that Hustle Harder."**
- **Visuals**: Dark mode accents, vibrant highlights (Pink, Cyan, Green, Yellow), fluid animations (Locomotive Scroll, GSAP), premium typography.
- **Tone**: Confident, direct, results-oriented. Focus on "Automation," "Speed," and "Revenue," not just "Design."

## 2. Proposed Site Structure

### A. New Pages (Recommended)
1.  **About Us (`/about`)**
    *   *Why*: The Home page "Keyhole" section teases this, but a dedicated page builds trust.
    *   *Content*:
        *   **The Philosophy**: "We don't just build pretty sites; we build revenue engines."
        *   **The Team**: Show the humans behind the automation.
        *   **The Stack**: Highlight the tech (React, GSAP, AI tools) as a competitive advantage.
    *   *Design*: Kinetic typography, team photos with hover effects (glitch or color shift).

2.  **Case Studies / Work (`/work` or enhance `/gallery`)**
    *   *Why*: "Real results from real clients" needs proof.
    *   *Content*:
        *   **Deep Dives**: 3-4 detailed case studies.
        *   **Metrics**: "3x Leads," "90% less admin time" (tie back to Home page testimonials).
        *   **Before/After**: Visual sliders.
    *   *Design*: Large imagery, sticky scrolling descriptions.

### B. Existing Page Refinements

3.  **Services (`/services`)**
    *   *Current State*: Exists in routes.
    *   *Refinement*: Structure around the 5 pillars from Home:
        1.  **Performance Web Dev**: "98+ Lighthouse scores."
        2.  **SEO & Content**: "Rank locally, dominate globally."
        3.  **AI Automation**: "Booking bots, CRM syncs, auto-replies."
        4.  **Digital Marketing**: "Ads that convert."
        5.  **Care Plans**: "Ship • Learn • Improve."
    *   *Design*: Bento-grid layout or horizontal scroll for each pillar.

4.  **Pricing (`/pricing`)**
    *   *Current State*: Exists in routes.
    *   *Refinement*:
        *   **"Sprint" Packages**: Fixed-price, fixed-scope builds (e.g., "The Launchpad," "The Growth Engine").
        *   **Care Plans**: Monthly retainers for SEO + Automation.
        *   **ROI Calculator**: Interactive element showing potential time/money saved with automation.
    *   *Design*: Comparison tables with "Recommended" cards that pop out on hover.

5.  **Resources / Playbook (`/resources`)**
    *   *Current State*: Exists in routes.
    *   *Refinement*:
        *   **The Playbook**: The "5 workflows that save 10+ hours" lead magnet from Home.
        *   **Blog**: Articles on "AI for Business," "SEO in 2025."
    *   *Design*: Magazine style, filterable by topic (Automation, SEO, Dev).

6.  **Contact (`/contact`)**
    *   *Current State*: Exists in routes.
    *   *Refinement*:
        *   **Primary CTA**: "Book a Discovery Call" (Calendar embed).
        *   **Secondary**: Simple form for quick inquiries.
        *   **FAQ**: Address common objections right before the form.
    *   *Design*: Split screen: Contact form on one side, "Why work with us" bullet points on the other.

## 3. Technical & Design Requirements
-   **Navigation**: Ensure the "Animated Navbar" (Aurora Theme) is consistent across all pages.
-   **Scroll**: Implement `useLocomotiveScroll` hook on ALL pages for consistent smooth scrolling.
-   **Transitions**: Page transition animations (e.g., curtain wipe or fade) to maintain the "premium app" feel.
-   **Footer**: Consistent footer with the "Steal our playbook" CTA on pages where it fits.

## 4. Implementation Priority
1.  **Services**: Critical for explaining *what* you actually do beyond the hype.
2.  **Contact**: The conversion point.
3.  **About**: Trust builder.
4.  **Work/Gallery**: Proof of competence.
5.  **Pricing**: Qualification filter.
